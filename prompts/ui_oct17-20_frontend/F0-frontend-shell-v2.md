You are rebuilding the frontend's foundation for the pivot ("Test your plans", SPEC §15). F1–F3 will build the screens on top of it in parallel, so **the store, the actions and the component slots you define are their contract.**

**Read these first:**
- `docs/EVENTS.md` v2: the contract, word for word
- `docs/DESIGN.md` §12 (screens), §11 (tokens, fonts, light only) and §4 (glossary)

**Rewrite:**
- `frontend/lib/events.ts`: TypeScript types for every v2 REST body and response and every SSE event, plus `SSE_EVENT_NAMES`. Keep `decodeChoicesB64`.
- `frontend/lib/store.ts` (Zustand). State:
  - `step` ("business" | "news" | "analyst" | "memo");
  - `examples`, `profile`, `profileStatus`;
  - `analysis` (id, mode, status);
  - `news`, `shocks`;
  - `plans` (in arrival order, with source and round), `crowdByPlan`;
  - `cells` (keyed `plan|shock`, cloud results replace local ones);
  - `summary`, `agentSteps`;
  - `reasoningByRound` (text accumulates, kept out of render-heavy paths);
  - `memo`, `telemetry`;
  - `replay` (open, planName, shockId, playing, week, ticks by week U1/U2, markers).

  **Actions:** `loadExamples`, `parseProfile(text)`, `setProfileField(key, value)`, `pickExample(id)`, `startAnalysis()`, `addPlan(spec)`, `newsCheck()`, `verify()`, `openReplay(planName, shockId)`, `closeReplay()`, `setStep(step)`, `downloadMemo()`.

  Only the arena reads `choices` from a ref (the same rule as before: at most one store update per week).
- `frontend/lib/stream.ts`: two EventSource clients (analysis stream, replay stream) with reconnect, using `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_BASE`. With no API base or an offline backend, fall back to the mock automatically, and show it in the mode chip.
- `frontend/lib/format.ts`: money per DESIGN.md §6 (`+$5.0k`, `−$1.8k` with a real minus, `$38.00`), weeks ("Week 7 of 24"), percentages.
- `frontend/mock/index.ts`: a scripted v2 stream for `coffee_hcmc`, using the same numbers as D7's `DATA`:
  - **Analysis:** about 25 s. Seed plans, 2 AI rounds, ~12 `agent_step`s, reasoning chunks, crowd for each plan, all `grid_cell`s, `grid_summary`, the memo, telemetry, `done`.
  - **Replay:** 24 weeks of plausible U1/U2 ticks (with `choices_b64` for 200 consumers) and the markers from D7, generated by a small seeded function.
  - Also mock REST: `/api/examples` (3 examples), `/api/profile/parse` (returns the example profile after 1.5 s), add plan (returns plausible cells), news-check (one live card).
- `frontend/app/page.tsx`, `frontend/app/globals.css`, `frontend/tailwind.config.ts`: the shell.
  - Top bar: name, step bar, mode chip, credibility chip from `telemetry`, and the "Powered by NVIDIA Nemotron" mark.
  - The current step's screen.
  - The replay drawer over the analyst screen.
  - Tokens and fonts exactly as DESIGN.md §11.
  - Keyboard: `Space` play/pause in the replay, `←/→` step a week, `Esc` closes the replay.
  - Works at 1440×900, 1280×800 and 390 px wide.
